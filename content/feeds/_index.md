Unofficial feeds for sites I follow that don't offer the RSS feed I want. Paste a feed's XML URL into your RSS reader to subscribe. My own writing has a [separate blog feed](/blog/index.xml).

{{< feed-list >}}

These feeds are checked every six hours and when this site is deployed. GitHub's scheduler can run late. If a source is unavailable or its layout changes, the last successful feed remains available and its status is shown here. Entries link to the original publisher; these feeds aren't affiliated with the source sites.

The [source and instructions for adding another feed](https://github.com/shkku/shkku.github.io/tree/main/feeds) are public.
